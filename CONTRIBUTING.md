# Contributing

## Entry format
`| ✓ | Title (link) | Venue/Year | Type tags | TL;DR (one line) |`

## Inclusion policy
- Peer-reviewed venue or high-quality preprint
- On-topic to one of the collections
- `🕓` on ingest → human promotes to `✅`

## Exclusion
- Off-scope, duplicate, or unverifiable claims
